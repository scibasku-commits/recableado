## Scope and visitor mode

Read-first learning hub at `/aprender/` and `/en/learn/`, plus its entry points from the shared navigation and home pages.

## Audience, job and action

An adult who is curious about AI but wary of jargon must immediately understand where to begin. The primary action is entering the eight-episode AI Costa course; the Cheat Sheet and Giora's exercises are supporting resources.

## Proof and constraints

The hub uses only existing, inspectable resources. It preserves the current URLs of AI Costa, the Cheat Sheet and the exercises. Spanish and English must remain equivalent and accessible on mobile and keyboard.

## Direction and memorable moment

An editorial gateway inside the existing Recableado world. AI Costa dominates the first viewport as a dark navy course doorway with a large eight-episode count; the two supporting resources follow as precise rows rather than competing cards.

## Unresolved decisions

None for the local candidate. Publication remains separately gated by Giora.
