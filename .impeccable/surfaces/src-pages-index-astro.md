## Scope and mode

Prototype of the Spanish homepage and one Spanish article. Visitor mode: Read, with the homepage acting as the editorial entrance.

## Audience, job and action

Readers aged roughly 50 to 70 should recognize Giora as a real person, understand what the newest story is about, open it, and continue reading without strain.

## Content and proof

Use the real article metadata and the existing 038 hero asset until the new portrait treatment arrives. Keep the chatbot, archive route, language switch, reactions and sharing functional. Missing images need a deliberate typographic frame.

## Constraints

Local preview only. No merge or deployment. Do not include the subscriber-form branch. Body text is at least 18 px, contrast is high, mobile width is safe, and reduced-motion preferences remove the reveal.

## Chosen direction

Sala oscura: true black with restrained texture, nearly monochrome imagery and one ember-orange accent. The homepage opens on the latest story at viewport scale; previous stories form a smaller edit strip. The article stages its title over the opening image and uses large editorial pauses, quotes and numbered scene changes instead of decorative effects.

## Memorable moment

The first story feels like a frame chosen by an editor: Giora's working scene fills the screen while the title sits low and left, then the page cuts cleanly into a quiet, highly legible reading column.
