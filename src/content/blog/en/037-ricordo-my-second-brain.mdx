Back in May I introduced you to my new partner. It was called Littlebird, it lived in my Mac, it saw what I was doing and — for the first time — I didn't have to explain everything again every morning. I wrote about it here: [«I no longer travel the Sixth Continent alone»](/en/blog/024-littlebird-nuevo-socio).

Three months on I still think the same: it solved my real problem, which was never intelligence, it was **context**. With a normal AI you explain who you are every time you open the window. It's like having a partner with amnesia.

But two things have happened.

## The first: I read the small print. Again

Twenty dollars a month. Two hundred and forty a year. So that a program can remember what **I** do on my own computer.

And in March they raised eleven million dollars.

Good for them, genuinely. But eleven million isn't a gift: it's investors. And investors, sooner or later, want their money back. That's called raising the price, changing the plans, or selling the company to somebody who never asked me anything.

Meanwhile, where is my memory? In their house.

## The second: this had already happened to me

In July I told you I'd done the same thing with dictation — [Háblame](/en/blog/033-i-built-my-own-dictation-app), 15 dollars a month turned into a tool of my own with a one-time price. And in that post I wrote a line that kept coming back to me:

> *Digital sovereignty can now be wired up in an afternoon.*

Well, you don't get to say that once and leave it there. Either it's true both times, or it was never true at all.

So on 21 July I got to work. Eight days later there was a version that worked.

## What Ricordo is

The same thing my partner did, but living in my house.

**Ricordo sees what happens on your screen and remembers for you.** You ask it "what was the name of that hotel I looked at on Tuesday?" or "what did that supplier say about the taxes?", and it finds it. Not because you saved it anywhere: because it was in front of your eyes and Ricordo was watching.

It's a second brain. The first one, at 73, is already carrying quite a load.

## What it doesn't do, which is the important part

**Nothing it sees leaves your computer.** Not the captures, not the text, not your history. Capture is local and search is local. There's a **100% offline mode** you can check yourself — unplug the wifi and it keeps working.

If you turn on advanced AI, the only thing that travels is **the question you just asked**. Never the history. Never what you saw yesterday.

And there's no subscription. **49 € once.** Thirty-day free trial, no card. Mac, Windows and Linux.

Do the maths with me, it's easy: 240 dollars a year against 49 euros once. Over three years, about 720 dollars against 49.

## What this means (and again, it isn't the app)

I could tell you mine is better. It isn't, not at everything — they have eleven million dollars and I have a bicycle and a BMW K1200.

What Ricordo has and they structurally cannot have is simpler: **it's yours**. There's no company in the middle that can raise the price, shut down, get acquired, or decide one Tuesday that your plan no longer exists. You buy it, you install it, and the conversation is over.

That's Recableado, and by now it isn't a slogan: it's two tools.

One remembers what you **say**. The other remembers what you **see**. Neither sends anything anywhere.
